Lunar First Light

Challenge: Lunar Landing Site Navigator

Preston Eaton and Preston Jordan Jr.

Problem

South-polar planners need Sun and Earth positions above the local terrain horizon, for a chosen site, date, and mast height.

Method and NASA data

  • LOLA LDEM_75S_240M plus PGDA Site01 and Site20.
  • SPICE DE421 in the MOON_ME frame.
  • Checked against JPL Horizons quantity 4.

Two-site result

Connecting Ridge and Mons Mouton, 1–8 November 2026, 2 m mast. Fractions and interruptions are in the downloaded report, with the dataset hashes.

Geometry and engineering

Sunlight visibility is not electrical power. Earth visibility is not a radio link. The six-point model, Earth-orbit worksheet, and STONE scenario are labeled assumptions.

Team and next steps

Keep the same engine. Add higher-resolution near-field horizons, a radio link budget, and Accorria production receipts only after that service issues them.
